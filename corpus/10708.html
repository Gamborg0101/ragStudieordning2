1. Om uddannelsen

1.1 Uddannelsens faglige retning og vigtigste fagområder

Aims and objectives of the Master's programme

The overall purpose of the Master in Curating is to encourage students to work across a broad range of disciplines, and draw upon diverse methodologies that arise from active research in the curatorial field. It addresses the need in Denmark for academic training in curating, and at the same time fosters the transference of skills to broader contexts of cultural management, arts administration, and mediation practices that together help to sustain the cultural and economic vitality of society at large. This has relevance to a broad interdisciplinary cultural field, and is aimed at those working in, and wanting to work in, cultural institutions, including galleries, museums, archives, festivals, events, publications and online platforms. It fosters broad competences in cultural analysis, communications, management and enterprise allowing students to operate within and across a range of contemporary institutions that organize, manage, administer and disseminate displays and events to audiences. Students are encouraged to reflect on methodological and theoretical dimensions of curating, understood as conceptual, artistic, discursive, educational, historical, technical and institutional practices. Drawing on diverse practices that arise from active research in the curatorial field, students work across a broad range of disciplines, as well as utilize national and international networks.


1.2 Kompetenceprofil

Knowledge and understanding

Master's candidate in Curating has knowledge about:

  • Relevant theories, concepts and methodologies in curatorial research at national and international levels.

A Master's candidate in Curating has developed an understanding that enables their:

  • Critical reflection on the theoretical and conceptual tools they use as well as scientific problems related to curating
  • Analysis of a given problem from interdisciplinary positions and assessment of the impact of the different perspectives
  • Analysis and evaluation of existing key issues and concepts related to the curatorial field by placing them in historical, social and cultural context

Skills

A Master's candidate working independently, and in collaboration with others, can:

  • Use appropriate theories, concepts and methodologies from both international and national research on curating, and apply knowledge of how they can be applied in relation to complex issues and diverse situations
  • Apply and generate interdisciplinary curatorial knowledge in a variety of contexts
  • Evaluate and analyse key issues and formulate possible solutions based on academic knowledge
  • Apply research-based knowledge in their practice, and participate in scientific debate in the field
  • Demonstrate the ability to communicate issues related to the curatorial field to a broad constituency - both to peers and non-specialist audiences

Competences

A Master's candidate, working independently and in collaboration with others, is able to:

  • Evaluate situations, problem-solve and find solutions based on practical and academic knowledge of the field
  • Solve tasks in a professional and ethical manner within relevant areas of curatorial work, related to organizational, technical and practical aspects
  • Make their own practice the subject of reflection and analysis using a range of interdisciplinary tools related to curating
  • Contribute to the development of the field of curatorial practice and curatorial knowledge.

1.3 Adgangskrav og forudsætninger

Admission requirements (regarding education)
Admission to the degree programme requires applicants to have completed at least one of the following degree programmes:

  • A relevant bachelor's or master's degree, for example in the social sciences or humanities (e.g. Art History or Aesthetics and Culture)
  • A relevant professional or vocational higher education (e.g. school teacher or educator) or
  • A relevant diploma (e.g. Diploma in Management - Arts and Culture)
It is also a requirement that the applicant have at least B level in English.

The university may admit applicants who do not fulfil the conditions in section 1.3, but who, on the basis of a specific academic assessment, are deemed to have comparable educational qualifications.

1.4 Adgangskrav (Erhvervserfaringsmæssige)

Admission requirements (regarding work experience)
Admission to the degree programme requires applicants to have at least two years of relevant work experience after completing the degree programme on which admission is based.

Relevant occupations include the following:

  • Staff at museums, galleries and other cultural institutions (across exhibition, education and administration aspects)
  • Arts and cultural workers
  • Independent curators
  • Archivists
  • Managers, administrators and coordinators (e.g. in connection with festivals and events)
  • Communication specialists and technologists who work in the cultural field
  • Interaction designers
  • Educators (e.g. high school)
  • Artists
  • Designers

No exemption will be granted on the basis of work experience.

Prior to admission, the university reserves the right to carry out a compulsory supervisory interview with applicants.


1.5 Ramme for uddannelsens færdiggørelse

The degree programme must be completed within six years of the enrolment. The Master's project concludes the degree programme. Students must complete each single module no later than two years after having participated in the teaching in the single module. The period is calculated at the end of the semester, including the re-examination period.

Modul 1

15 ECTS
Curatorial Theories 15 ECTS 1. semester 7-trinsskala
Prøvedetaljer Se kursusudbud

Kvalifikationsbeskrivelse:

Aim: 

This course introduces a range of theoretical positions to open up an understanding of the interdisciplinary field of curating. Its central purpose is to ensure a common ground for students coming from diverse backgrounds and then to use this as a foundation to share experiences and working perspectives amongst the group through peer learning and exchange.

Objectives:

On completion of the course, students will have knowledge of: relevant theories, concepts and methodologies related to the broad field of curating across a broad range of interdisciplinary research fields, and in both national an international contexts; understanding of interdisciplinary positions sufficient to allow them to perform an analysis of a given problem. They will be able to demonstrate: their ability to analyse and evaluate existing key issues and concepts related to the curatorial field by placing them in wider historical, social and cultural contexts informed by interdisciplinary perspectives. They will also be able to evaluate and analyse key issues related to curating based on their academic knowledge of interdisciplinary fields. This will enable them to reflect and analyse a range of interdisciplinary tools related broadly to the curatorial field.

Karakterbeskrivelse:

Students are examined on their ability to:

  • Demonstrate an understanding of interdisciplinary positions sufficient to perform an analysis of a given problem.
  • Evaluate and analyse key issues related to curating based on their academic knowledge of interdisciplinary fields.

Indhold:

Content: 

The course establishes the trans-disciplinary field of curatorial studies by building on the student group’s previous academic and professional experience, and then by introducing some formative theoretical ideas from cultural theory, art history, digital aesthetics, cultural policy and education theory, taking a thematic focus on issues of contemporaneity, participation and collaboration.

Modul 2

15 ECTS
Curatorial Practice 15 ECTS 2. semester 7-trinsskala
Prøvedetaljer Se kursusudbud

Kvalifikationsbeskrivelse:

Aim:

The course requires students to initiate a case study report based on their experiences of field trips, residencies (or perhaps based on a curatorial project within their own institution in special cases). The purpose is to encourage a close analysis of a curatorial project in its institutional and cultural context, and to fully analyse the broader ecology of influences, logistical constraints, and practical competences that are involved in the realization. This is also the place where the core practical aspects of curating related to project management, budget management and administration are introduced.

Objectives: 

On completion of the course, students will have gained knowledge related to: the analysis of a given problem from interdisciplinary positions and the assessment of the impact of these different perspectives in practice. They will be able to: apply knowledge of how interdisciplinary positions can be applied in relation to the complexity of issues that arise in practice and diverse working situations; evaluate and analyse key issues and formulate possible solutions based on academic knowledge of the curatorial and related fields. This will enable them to evaluate situations, problem-solve and find solutions based on practical and academic knowledge of the curatorial and related fields.

Karakterbeskrivelse:

Students are examined on their ability to:

  • Perform an analysis of a given problem from interdisciplinary positions and to assess  the impact of these different perspectives in practice.
  • Evaluate situations, problem-solve and find solutions based on practical and academic knowledge of the curatorial and related fields.

Indhold:

Content:

The course includes a series of visits to relevant institutions and projects, from which a report is prepared that examines practical aspects of curatorial work (including the ways that decisions are arrived at, schedules are developed, problems identified and solved, and compromises agreed in order to balance the competing agendas of the various actors involved). It is expected that the report will include documentation, and supporting materials (such as interviews) and this aspect of the course is supported by a number of workshops that introduce students to technical and communication skills (e.g. using social and internet technologies, interview techniques).

Modul 3

15 ECTS
Curatorial Knowledge 15 ECTS 3. semester 7-trinsskala
Prøvedetaljer Se kursusudbud

Kvalifikationsbeskrivelse:

Aim:

The course offers an in-depth analysis of curatorial practice and the interdisciplinary discourse that has developed around it in recent years. The purpose of the course is to expose students to current debates and discussions, and to test these ideas in the broader research environment of curatorial praxis in an expanded sense – involving both academics and those involved directly in curatorial projects. It aims to test the student’s ongoing investigations and their relevance to an international community of peers through an international symposium. In this way, it serves to extend the networks of the students and the programme as a whole, and feeds into wider research ambitions of the team.

Objectives: 

On completion of the course, students will have knowledge of: relevant theories, concepts and methodologies in current curatorial research on an international stage; the theoretical and conceptual tools employed as well as scientific problems related to curating. They will be able to: apply research-based knowledge critically in their practice, and participate in relevant scientific discussions; demonstrate the ability to communicate issues related to the curatorial field to a broad constituency - to their peers. This will enable them to participate in the debates about curatorial theory and practice, and contribute to the development of the field of curatorial knowledge.

Karakterbeskrivelse:

Students are examined on their ability to:

  • Apply research-based knowledge critically in their practice, and participate in relevant scientific discussions.
  • Communicate issues related to the curatorial field to a broad constituency, including peers.

Indhold:

Content:

The module will be organized around the planning and delivery of an international symposium, produced in collaboration with similar curatorial initiatives and/or with other partner institutions. Students will be expected to take an active role in panels, papers, and the organizational aspects of the event. Thematic strands will arise from the concerns of the group and the interdisciplinary discourse around curating, with a particular focus on the core distinction between curating and the curatorial as a new knowledge domain.

Modul 4

15 ECTS
Curatorial Project (Master's Project) 15 ECTS 4. semester 7-trinsskala
Prøvedetaljer Se kursusudbud

Kvalifikationsbeskrivelse:

Aim:

The course requires students to develop an ambitious, self-initiated individual or collaborative curatorial project for public dissemination. Its purpose is to synthesize what has been learnt thus far in the other courses (that are more formative in character): including curatorial theory, curatorial practice and curatorial knowledge. It consolidates specialist concerns and to actualize student’s curatorial ambitions. 

Objectives: 

On completion of the course, students will operate through: critical practice of curating, and critical reflection on the theoretical and conceptual tools related to the curatorial field. They will be able to: apply and generate interdisciplinary curatorial knowledge in a variety of practical and theoretical contexts; demonstrate the ability to communicate significant issues related to the curatorial field to a broad constituency - both to peers and non-specialist audiences alike. This will allow them to: solve tasks in a professional and ethical manner within relevant areas of curatorial practice, related to the organizational, technical and practical aspects of curating; establish their own practice and theoretical work as the subject of critical reflection and analysis using a range of interdisciplinary tools appropriate to the field of curating and to the development of their own position in the field.

Karakterbeskrivelse:

Students are examined on their ability to:

  • Communicate significant issues related to the curatorial field to a broad constituency, both to peers and non-specialist audiences alike.
  • Establish their own practice and theoretical work as the subject of critical reflection and analysis using a range of interdisciplinary tools appropriate to the field of curating and to the development of their own position in the field.

Indhold:

Content:

The course concentrates on the production of a documented curated project understood in its broadest sense (eg. an exhibition, performance, symposium, book, online project), supported by the production of a written paper (that is fully-referenced). Overall it draws upon aspects of the praxis of curating as developed through the overall study programme, including: conceptual development and planning, management and organization, selection of works, negotiations with artists/producers, and audience engagement, education and communication strategies, and so on.

3.3 Til- og afmelding af prøver

Tilmelding til fag, prøver og valgfag
Studerende bliver servicetilmeldt undervisning og eksamen i uddannelsens obligatoriske fag i den semesterrækkefølge, studieordningen foreskriver, når den studerende er optaget på hele uddannelsen. Studerende, der er optaget på et enkeltmodul, bliver servicetilmeldt undervisning og eksamen på enkeltmodulet. Den studerende er selv ansvarlig for til enhver tid at være korrekt tilmeldt fag og prøver. Den studerende skal selv søge optagelse på og eventuelt prioritere valgfag inden for den fastsatte tilmeldingsfrist. Efter tildeling af plads på valgfag og efter afmeldingsfristen udgør valgfaget en obligatorisk del af den studerendes uddannelse og kan ikke ændres.

Studerende skal selv tilmelde sig omprøven inden for den fastsatte frist, som fremgår af studieportalen. Afmelding foregår via den studerendes selvbetjening på mit.au.dk og følger den på studieportalen beskrevne procedure. Det er en forudsætning for tilmelding til omprøven, at den studerende har brugt et forsøg på ordinær eksamen forud for omprøven.
Framelding kan ikke finde sted efter afmeldingsfristen, og der er brugt et prøveforsøg, uanset om den studerende ikke deltager i prøven.

Ved bedømmelsen af masterprojekt og andre større skriftlige opgaver vil der ud over det faglige indhold også blive lagt vægt på den studerendes stave- og formuleringsevne.

3.7 Dispensationer

En dispensation er en afvigelse fra den eller de regler, der almindeligvis gælder for det pågældende område. Dispensation kan gives på baggrund af ansøgning til den myndighed, der har kompetencen til at give dispensation. Ansøgning om dispensation indgives til studienævnet. Hvis en anden myndighed har kompetencen, videresender studienævnet ansøgningen til rette myndighed (f.eks. dekan, rektor eller ministerium). En dispensationsansøgning skal være skriftlig og begrundet, og skal indgives hurtigst muligt. Hvis ansøgningen umiddelbart skal kunne behandles, skal den indeholde tydelig angivelse af, hvad det er for en regel der søges dispensation fra og hvad der ønskes opnået med dispensationen (f.eks. tilladelse til hjælpemidler, forlænget prøvetid, udsættelse af tidsgrænse). Ansøgningen skal vedlægges dokumentation for de usædvanlige forhold, der begrunder ansøgningen. Udokumenterede forhold kan sædvanligvis ikke tillægges betydning.Der henvises til studieportalen for yderligere information om dispensationer .

3.8 Anke og klage

Klage over prøver indgives til universitetet. Forudsætningen for at en klage umiddelbart kan behandles er, at den er skriftlig. Det skal fremgå af klagen, hvad der klages over, og hvad der ønskes opnået med klagen. Klager over prøver skal indgives senest 14 dage efter fristen for offentliggørelse af prøvens resultat jf. eksamensbekendtgørelsen. Der henvises til studieportalen for yderligere information om eksamensklager.
